17. Autoplay Control Button

Autoplay ControlPlay / PauseAutoplay

A play/pause toggle for an autoplaying slider. There is no attribute switching it on — the three waterfall-el elements below (autoplay-control, plus an autoplay-control-pause and an autoplay-control-play label) are the whole setup, and the library shows whichever label matches the current state. All three are required; leave one out and the control does nothing.

Slide 1
Slide 2
Slide 3
Slide 4
Slide 5
Slide 6

Autoplay: —

<div waterfall="control-slider" waterfall-autoplay="true" waterfall-autoplay-delay="1800"> <button waterfall-el="autoplay-control"> <span waterfall-el="autoplay-control-pause">Pause</span> <span waterfall-el="autoplay-control-play">Play</span> </button>
